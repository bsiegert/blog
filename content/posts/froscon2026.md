At [FrOSCon 2026](https://froscon.org/), I gave a talk titled

> [**Jiu Jitsu mit dem Quellcode**](https://programm.froscon.org/froscon2026/talk/f4a0f6ff-9247-4603-aaf2-f32ced841d58/)

I presented the [`jj`](https://jj-vcs.dev/) version control system and also
tried to make a connection to the ongoing maintenance crisis in open source.
After all, newcomers often cite `git` as a scary thing they have to learn.

The manuscript for my talk (in German) is now available at [/talks/froscon2026/](/talks/froscon2026/).

Thank you to everyone who listened and who participated in the interesting
discussion!

Thank you also to the single person who left a comment in the
evaluation system, saying that it was "rhetorically terrible" and "painful to
watch". So nice of you.
